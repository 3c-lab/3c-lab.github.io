This project follows the same multi-parametric MRI and single-cell whole-brain imaging approach as our [Alcohol Use Disorder project](/projects/1_alcohol/), applied here to oxycodone, in collaboration with the NIDA-funded Rat Oxycodone Biobank (U01DA051937) of Olivier George.

By combining longitudinal MRI with whole-brain cellular imaging in genetically diverse rats with fully characterized addiction-like behaviors, we aim to identify the brain features that predict vulnerability or resilience to opioid use disorder, both before and after exposure to oxycodone.
