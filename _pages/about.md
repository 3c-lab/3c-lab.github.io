Research group under the Preclinical Addiction Research Consortium in the Department of Psychiatry at UCSD, studying neurodevelopmental and substance use disorders, with the help of rodent neuroimaging.

The lab combines single-cell whole-brain imaging — using tissue clearing and light-sheet microscopy — with longitudinal in vivo magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) to capture both the cellular and systems-level architecture of the brain. This dual approach allows us to track how individual brains change over time, before and after disease or substance exposure, while also resolving the underlying cellular activity driving those changes.
