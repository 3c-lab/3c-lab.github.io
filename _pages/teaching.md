The 3c Lab is always interested in hearing from motivated students and researchers who want to contribute to our work on whole-brain imaging, individual differences, and neuropsychiatric disorders.

### Undergraduate & Post-Bac students
We welcome undergraduate and post-bac students interested in gaining hands-on research experience in neuroscience, behavioral assays, imaging, or computational analysis. No prior lab experience is required — curiosity, enthusiasm and reliability matter most, we'll help you grow and find purpose.

### Master & PhD students (rotations and visiting students)
We host master and PhD students for rotations or visiting research stays, including international exchange programs. Projects can be tailored to align with your thesis or program requirements.

### Postdoctoral positions
We are actively recruiting a postdoc for work on the alcohol brain imaging project. Also open to discussing postdoctoral opportunities with candidates whose research interests align with the lab's focus on whole-brain imaging, addiction neuroscience, or neurodevelopmental disorders.
