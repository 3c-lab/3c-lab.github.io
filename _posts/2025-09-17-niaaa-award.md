We are pleased to announce a Notice of Award from the **National Institute on Alcoholism and Alcohol Abuse (NIAAA)**.

**R01AA031958:** *Magnetic Resonance Imaging of Outbred Rats with Variable Vulnerability to Compulsive Alcohol Use*

This award will support our work using longitudinal multi-parametric MRI to characterize individual differences in brain features associated with vulnerability and resilience to alcohol use disorder in genetically diverse rats.
